# 🎵 The Legend of Zelda: 40th Anniversary Song Pack

Welcome! This repository features **32 custom songs** created to celebrate the **40th Anniversary of The Legend of Zelda** series. (The idea initially was to create 40 songs, but due to some issues with my left-hand, I had to slow down a little bit)

These tracks are designed for insertion into your own ROM Hacks and are pulled directly from my upcoming Pokémon ROM Hack, **"The Legend of Zelda: Minish Quest"**: an ambitious crossover bringing together the worlds of Zelda and Pokémon! We have tracks expanding through many eras of The Legend of Zelda series here.

---

## 🛠️ Quick Start & Integration Guide

* **Recommended Tool:** For the best insertion experience, I highly recommend using **[Porydaw](https://github.com/huderlem/porydaw)**. Just use Porydaw "Import Midi" functionality, review the Voice groups, build your ROM Hack and that's it!
* **Voice Group Customization:** Feel free to adjust the instruments to match your project's voice groups. These tracks were built using a limited number of **Voice Channels** to ensure smooth, hassle-free in-game importing.
* **File Size:** Each song takes up only about **0.02%** of your ROM space! You will have plenty of room to play around with and enjoy these tracks without worrying about hitting storage limits.
