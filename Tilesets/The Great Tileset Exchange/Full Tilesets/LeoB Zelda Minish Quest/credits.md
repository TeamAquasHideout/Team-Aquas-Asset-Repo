# The Legend of Zelda: Minish Quest

## What are these Tilesets?

They are part of my upcoming open-source ROM Hack called "The Legend of Zelda: Minish Quest". I made inspired by the amazing art from The Legend of Zelda Minish Cap, and made them free for use, so anyone who would like to develop ROM Hacks can use new Tilesets for their projects.

If you would like to contribute in creating tilesets with me, feel free to drop a message in Reddit/Discord! Also, I'll be very happy to see my Tilesets in use for your projects :D

Happy ROM Hacking everyone!
